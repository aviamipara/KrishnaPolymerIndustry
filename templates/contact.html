Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our polymer ordering process, custom certifications, and capabilities.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom sheets?

For standard stocked sheets (e.g., standard natural or black HDPE/PP panels), there is no MOQ. For custom extruded colors, dimensions, or specialized grades (such as antimicrobial or specific glass-filled formulas), our MOQ typically ranges from 500kg to 1 Ton per size, depending on our extrusion line configuration.

Can you supply custom extruded profiles or shapes?

Yes. We operate custom profile extrusion tooling. We can design custom dies based on your CAD drawings. For custom profile shapes, an initial tooling and die setup fee applies, along with specific MOQ runs.

Are your KrishTec® HDPE cutting boards FDA compliant?

Absolutely. Our KrishTec® Sanitary Cutting Board sheets are manufactured strictly with 100% virgin polymer resin and FDA-compliant dyes. They comply fully with FDA Food Regulation 21 CFR 177.1520 and NSF standards, making them suitable for commercial food processing facilities.

How do you control dimensional stress in compressed sheets?

All of our compressed UHMW and heavy-gauge plates undergo a slow, computer-controlled heat annealing cycle. This process relieves internal molecular stress, ensuring that the sheets do not bow, cup, or warp when you slice, route, or machine them into precise components.
