Corporate Profile

Geared Towards Polymer Packaging Excellence

Since our founding in 1998, Krishna Polymer Industry has grown from a specialized molding shop into a leading international manufacturer of high-performance PET packaging solutions, caps, and closures. Headquartered in the industrial hub of Gujarat, India, we serve critical sectors ranging from agrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, food & beverage, to specialized industrial chemicals.

Our commitment goes beyond simply molding plastics. We design and engineer packaging systems with advanced barrier properties, leak-proof designs, and UN-rated safety compliance. Partnering with global leaders, we deliver application-specific consulting, custom tooling, and eco-friendly PCR-PET options that optimize storage safety and shelf-life.

Agro-PET Packaging

Engineered for high chemical compatibility. Our bottles feature triple-layer barrier technology to prevent chemical permeation, structural deformation, and leakage of volatile agro-chemicals.

UN-Rated Certified High-Barrier Resins Zero Permeability

Secure Closures & Caps

High-precision injection molded caps utilizing advanced induction heat seals (IHS). Our systems guarantee hermetic sealing, tamper evidence, and child-resistant locking mechanism safety.

Induction Sealed Tamper-Evident Ring Child-Resistant (CRC)

Sustainable PCR-PET

Pioneering the circular economy. We manufacture packaging containing up to 100% Post-Consumer Recycled PET, meeting rigid global food-grade standards while reducing carbon footprints by over 60%.

100% Recyclable FDA Food-Grade Approved 60% CO2 Reduction
Production Infrastructure

Our Manufacturing Facility

We operate all blow molding, injection molding, and quality assurance processes from our unified, fully-integrated plant in Ankleshwar, Gujarat.

Krishna Polymer Industries (Ankleshwar)

Our main manufacturing plant and corporate headquarters. This state-of-the-art facility houses our automatic stretch blow molding lines, high-speed closure injection lines, cleanroom assembly zones, and QA laboratories.

By consolidating all divisions under one roof, we achieve maximum logistics efficiency, seamless quality control, and rapid order fulfillment for our agricultural, pharmaceutical, and chemical packaging clients.
